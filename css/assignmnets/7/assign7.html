Popular Destinations

Goa

Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches, amazing sunsets, and
delicious local food.

PLACES TO VISIT

  • Baga Beach
  • Fort Aguda
  • Dudhsagar Falls

Manali

Manali offers snow-covered mountains, peaceful valleys, and
exciting adventure activities.

PLACES TO VISIT

  • Salang Valley
  • Rohtang Pass
  • Hadimba Temple

Jaipur

Jaipur is known for its royal palaces, historic forts, and colorful
traditional markets.

PLACES TO VISIT

  • Hawa Mahal
  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace
